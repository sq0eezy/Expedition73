Method and limitations

We fit a straight line to 24 annual means for each whole, unmasked region. Each annual mean uses all 12 monthly values, weighted by calendar days. The paired temperature analysis fits the same model to the annual differences.

Approximate 95% confidence intervals use Newey–West standard errors with two annual lags and a small-sample adjustment. An interval that includes zero leaves the trend direction statistically inconclusive under this method. The intervals describe uncertainty under the fitted model.

Northern Alaska is above the latitude limit for MERRA-2's observation-based precipitation correction over land. Each precipitation slope describes the recorded product in its region.

How to read the grid

Select a cell with the mouse or press Tab and use the arrow keys. The solid outline shows the candidate boundary and dashed outlines show complete grid cells. The diagram uses latitude and longitude coordinates.
